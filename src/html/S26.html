Connect Tech Inc. - Summer 2026

Between the months of April and September of 2026, I spent my time, once again, at Connect Tech Inc. as a Web Application Developer. During this time, I got the chance to dig deeper with my tasks, get more involved in the company, and make more meaningful changes thanks to my existing trust with the staff. I learned a lot, and had a great time.

Connect Tech Inc: Who Are They?

Connect Tech is a hardware design and manufacturing company that specializes in embedded computing solutions, with a heavy emphasis on solutions involving AI. They design and manufacture all of their products in-house! At the time of writing, they are undergoing very rapid growth, they have currently have over 10 open positions across their departments (It doesn't seem like much, but with a smaller local company that is pretty significant), and I got to play an active role in helping the new talent settle in.

Connect Tech HQ.

I previously mentioned that Connect Tech is a very promising company with a bright future, and my words came true. During my time there, over 30 employees were hired, the president of the company, Patrick Dietrich got named as the most innovative man by a very reputable embedded computing magazine, Embedded Computing Design. They are a gem to watch out for in embedded computing world!
